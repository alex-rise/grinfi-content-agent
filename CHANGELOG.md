# What changed

Newest first. Plain language: this file is read out to the person using the kit,
not to a developer. Update it in the same commit that changes behaviour.

## 14 October 2026

- **The agent opens the session.** On the first message it reads the client
  folder, says what it knows and what is missing, and names one next step,
  instead of asking an open question.
- **Mining starts with an inventory.** Before asking a founder to record
  anything, search what already exists - their channel, past webinars, calls
  already recorded. The ask is the last step now, not the first.
- **A correction to the mining rule.** It claimed a meeting recap had fabricated
  a case. It had not: the meeting had a second part that the transcript did not
  cover. Absence from the material you hold is not evidence of invention.
- **Reviewing posts that already exist** is now part of the `writing` skill: what
  to do when someone arrives with a stack of posts written by a previous tool or
  agency.
- **Updates reach you by themselves** - see "Staying current" in `CLAUDE.md`.

## 13 October 2026

- **The shape of a post now comes from the client's `voice.md`, not from the
  kit.** A voice that ships short broken lines and meaningful emoji markers was
  being rewritten into tidy paragraphs, and the approver read the paragraphs as
  machine text.
- **The mirror-pair ban covers the whole family** ("it's not X. It's Y.", "not
  because X. Because Y.") with a search-and-rewrite instruction, plus more
  openers, escalators, and curly quotes.
- **A number from outside carries what it actually counts**, not only its source.
- **"See more" is no longer sold as a click that counts:** what is reported to
  count is whether the reader stays. The 2026 reports on links in the body
  disagree, so that one is a default to test, not a law.
- **Rules for reposts from the team and for comments** on other people's posts.
- A Windows render command for images and carousels.
- The kit's own examples broke the kit's own rules and are rewritten.

## 6 October 2026

- First version: six skills (onboarding, mining, planning, writing, visuals,
  publishing), the platform and materials references, three annotated example
  posts with image briefs, an image template with four layouts, and a carousel
  template that renders both a LinkedIn PDF and Instagram slides.
